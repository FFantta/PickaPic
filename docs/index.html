PickaPic Privacy

Local edition · October 31, 2026

PickaPic stores your journal photos, captions, calendar dates and framing preferences in the app's local storage. This edition has no accounts, application servers, advertising SDKs or remote analytics.

Permissions

Camera access is requested when taking a photo. The system photo picker supplies images you choose. Saving a copy to Photos requests add-only authorization. Notifications are optional and contain no journal text or photographs.

Backups and exports

iOS may back up app data according to your device settings. Full backup packages contain private, unencrypted journal content. You choose where to save them. PDFs and photo collages are rendered locally and passed to another service only through your explicit use of system sharing. JPEG exports omit original location metadata. Removing the app may remove its local data.

Widgets and deletion

The Home Screen widget receives only your page count and today's completion state, not photos or captions. Deleted journal pages can be restored for 30 days. Permanent deletion does not erase separately exported backups, local recovery packages or Photos copies.
